Metastasis-free survival after radiotherapy and ADT, by PSMA PET staging and clinical factors

Five-factor Cox model fitted on 982 non-metastatic very-high-risk prostate cancer patients from 21 Indian centres treated with definitive radiotherapy and long-term ADT without abiraterone or docetaxel (243 MFS events, median follow-up 51 months). Research prototype for internal review; not validated externally.

Reference categories: conventional staging, ≤T3b, cN0, GG 4, prostate-only RT. Centre-clustered robust CIs are slightly narrower (pelvic RT 0.69–1.08). Apparent C-index 0.645; bootstrap optimism-corrected 0.636 (300 resamples). Calibration at 5 years by risk tertile: predicted 86 / 77 / 62% vs observed 83 / 76 / 65%. Proportional hazards hold for all terms (pelvic RT borderline, p=0.048).

Two questions this cohort answers

  • 1. What does PSMA PET staging do to prognosis? Full cohort, 1,087 patients (abiraterone/docetaxel recipients included and adjusted for): adjusted MFS HR 0.57 (0.42–0.76), distant metastasis 0.55 (0.39–0.76), overall survival 0.61 (0.38–0.96). It is a staging-quality effect, not a treatment effect: PSMA removes occult M1 disease from the "M0" group. The effect is concentrated in GG ≤4 (cN1 GG ≤4: 5-yr MFS 74% → 86%) and absent in GG5 (cN0 76 vs 77%; cN1 57 vs 63%).
  • 2. Which PSMA-staged patients can avoid systemic intensification? PSMA-staged STAMPEDE-eligible patients on RT + ADT alone (n=438, median follow-up 41 months): GG ≤4 reach 86% 5-yr / 81% 6-yr MFS, matching the STAMPEDE abiraterone arm; GG5 reach 66% / 63%, matching the STAMPEDE control arm; GG5 + T4 + cN1 only 43%. Grade group, not T or N, decides.
  • Caveats. Follow-up in the PSMA group is 41 months (91 at risk at 5 years); STAMPEDE's benchmark is from conventionally staged UK patients and its hazard ratio may not transport; grade is local biopsy grading from 21 centres; T stage below T4 does not separate because no patient entered on T2/T3 alone.
Prototype built for hypothesis generation. Before clinical use it needs external validation, a competing-risk check for non-prostate death, and centre-level calibration.

Computation

S(t | x) = S₀(t) ^ exp( Σ βᵢ · (xᵢ − x̄ᵢ) ) — S₀(t) is the Breslow baseline at the covariate means (lifelines CoxPHFitter); S₀(60 mo) = 0.769

Time runs from the first day of radiotherapy. MFS event = radiologically detected distant metastasis or death from any cause, whichever first. Cohort: 1,103 unique patients from the pooled cT4, cN1 and GG5 I-GURU registries after de-duplication; 15 excluded for missing RT or follow-up dates, 1 for unknown staging, 96 for abiraterone/docetaxel; 9 further dropped for missing T stage or grade.
